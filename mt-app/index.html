MT App

A private money tracker for budgets, bills, savings goals, receipts and crypto. No sign-up, works offline, and everything stays on your phone.

Download for Android What's new

Android 7.0 or newer · Free · No account needed

What it does

Accounts

Cash, bank, e-wallets (GCash, Maya), savings and credit cards, with your net worth at a glance.

Safe to spend today

How much you can spend each day until your next payday, after bills.

Budgets

Monthly limits per category, following your payday or the 15th/30th, with rollover of leftovers.

Bills and reminders

Recurring bills, credit card due dates and installments, with notifications before they're due.

Scan receipts

Take a photo of a receipt and the total is filled in for you, read on the phone itself.

Crypto

Track coins like BNB, USDT or XRP; the value updates to the latest price when you open the app.

Goals and utang

Savings goals with a monthly target, and money lent to or borrowed from people.

Reports and widget

Spending by category, net worth over 12 months, a monthly recap, CSV export, and a home-screen widget.

Getting started

  1. Install the newest mt_app_<version>.apk from the download folder (see how testing works).
  2. Set up your accounts and balances on the first run, or restore a backup.
  3. Turn on automatic backups in Settings; they're saved in Download/MT App.

Updating: open the new APK and tap Update; your data stays. Don't uninstall the old version first: uninstalling deletes everything the app has saved on your phone. Making a backup before updating is a good habit.

Your data

  • Everything is saved only on your phone. The app has no account and no server.
  • The only thing it gets from the internet is crypto prices from CoinGecko, and only if you track crypto. It sends just the coin names (e.g. "BNB"), never your amounts or other data.
  • Backups are files you control. Copy them to Drive or a computer now and then, since a lost or reset phone takes its files with it.
