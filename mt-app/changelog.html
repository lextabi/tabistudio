What's new in MT App

Every version for testers, newest first. Download the APK from the download folder; the file name has the version in it (mt_app_<version>.apk). Each version lists its SHA-256 checksum, so you can check that your download is the real file.

0.3.2 (September 29, 2026)

Download: mt_app_0.3.2.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 2627b03f5452ddf7fae9536b4f4a5bd25c5d3b501fe2fb28f1af9d0a5a475424

New: transfer fees

  • A transfer now has an optional Transfer fee field, e.g. ₱1,000 plus a ₱10 InstaPay fee.
  • Only the ₱1,000 reaches the other account. The ₱10 is taken from the account you sent from and saved as an expense under Transaction Fees (the category is added the first time you use it), so it shows in your spending and reports.

New: built-in calculator

  • Tap the calculator icon in an amount field, work it out (+ − × ÷ %), and tap Use to fill in the result. Also on the Home screen's top bar.
  • 1000 + 10% gives 1,100, like a phone calculator, and there are no rounding errors.

Fixed: app lock

  • With App lock on, coming back to the app showed "MT App is locked" and the Unlock button did nothing. Now the fingerprint prompt opens by itself when you come back, and Unlock always tries again.

Updating from 0.3.1: open the new APK and tap Update. Your data is kept. Don't uninstall first.

0.3.0 (September 27, 2026)

Download: mt_app_0.3.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 79c8fb7f9dd8d96b562f5583c23c59a9aa507c5a3b6eb808e5b296d1eda5cfe7

New: crypto accounts

  • Add a Crypto account (e.g. Binance) and the coins you hold, like 1 BNB. Search by name or symbol; the list shows each coin's rank so you pick the right one (several coins share symbols like XRP).
  • The value follows live prices from CoinGecko: updated when you open the app, when you come back to it and every 2 minutes while it's open. Pull down to update right away.
  • See the 24-hour change, and your gain or loss if you enter what you paid.
  • Crypto counts in your net worth (and its chart), but not in Safe to spend.
  • Offline, the last prices stay with the time they're from.
  • Coins not on CoinGecko can be added with your own price.

Privacy: the app now uses the internet, only to get crypto prices. It sends just the coin names, never your amounts.

0.2.0 (September 27, 2026)

Download: mt_app_0.2.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 6622add69138bb0461a81c853ab2770e81e38a81f7103cdb283fe60a34e645e9

New look

  • New app icon and splash screen.
  • Fresh design: a gradient balance card with frosted glass, a see-through bottom bar with a big + button, numbers that count up, and a deeper dark mode.
  • Swipe a transaction right to edit it, left to delete it.

New features

  • First-run setup: a new install asks for your real accounts and balances, or restores a backup.
  • Scan receipts: fills in the total and the store name from a photo.
  • Home-screen widget: safe to spend today and net worth, with a + button to add an expense.
  • Net worth over time: a 12-month chart in Reports and a small trend line on Home.
  • Monthly recap: how your last budget cycle went, offered on Home when a new cycle starts.
  • Subscription finder: spots bills you pay every month and turns them into reminders.
  • No-spend days: marked on the calendar, with your current and best streak.
  • Automatic backups: daily, weekly or monthly to Download/MT App, plus an optional backup password.

Updating from 0.1.0: open the new APK and tap Update. Your data is kept. Don't uninstall first.
