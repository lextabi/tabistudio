Calm Crew Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Calm Crew is a learning app for Android made by Tabi Studio ("we"), designed for children and for the grown-ups who set it up. In short: Calm Crew collects nothing about you or your child. It has no account, no ads, no purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no links out, and it makes no network requests. Nothing leaves the device.

What the app saves, and where

InformationWhere it stays
Settings (sounds, voice, vibration, Calm mode) In the app's private storage on the device only. We never receive it.
Pip's garden (how many flowers have grown) In the app's private storage on the device only. We never receive it.

That is all. The app doesn't ask for a name, email address, age or photo, and it never records what a child says. The only thing it keeps about play is how many flowers have grown in Pip's garden. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising ID.

Permissions

The only permission the app uses is vibration, for a tiny buzz when a child taps. A grown-up can switch it off in Settings. It never uses the microphone, camera or location.

Calm Crew does not have the Internet permission: the app, its pictures, fonts and voices all ship inside it, and it cannot connect to the internet. Android backup is also turned off for the app, so its settings and garden are not copied to any online backup.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We have nothing to share, because the app sends us nothing. There are no ad networks, analytics services or other third parties in the app. The app is installed from a file we provide through Google Drive, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting what's saved

In Settings (hold the gear button for 3 seconds), Clear the garden resets Pip's garden. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on the device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

Children

Calm Crew is made for children, including children on the autism spectrum. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to collect from a child, and no account for a child to create. Settings are behind a parent gate (hold the gear button for 3 seconds).

Changes

If we ever change this policy, for example if a future version adds something that uses the internet, we'll update the date at the top and say so on the app's page before that version is released.
