Calm Crew

A calm, sensory-friendly learning app for children on the autism spectrum, and for any child who likes a calm pace. Pip, Mochi and Tocky guide short activities in counting, spelling, speech, calming down and the day's routine.

What's in the app

The home screen is a sunny valley. Each activity is a place; a child taps one to go in. Three friendly characters, all original, host the places and speak in their own voices.

Counting Garden with Pip

Plant flowers one at a time while Pip counts aloud.

Word Treehouse with Pip

Tap letters in order to spell a pictured word. A wrong letter just wiggles and waits.

Tocky's Echo Shop with Tocky

Listen to a word, clap its syllables and say it back, in short rounds of three pictured words. The child taps "I said it!": the app never listens or records.

Mochi's Cozy Cave with Mochi

A slow breathing bubble, soft bubble popping and gentle rain, for calming down.

My Day with Tocky

A picture schedule with a visual countdown that warns before a change.

Pip's garden

Every finished activity grows a flower in Pip's garden. The reward is always the same and can't be lost, and the child chooses what happens next.

Character voices

Pip, Mochi and Tocky each have their own voice, so the app can be used without reading. Every spoken line ships with the app, so it works offline.

Calm mode and parent gate

Calm mode softens the colors and turns off movement, except the slow breathing bubble. Settings opens only when a grown-up holds the gear button for 3 seconds.

Designed to be calm

  • Predictable. The same layout and flow every time. Each activity has a clear start and end, and changes come with a warning.
  • Gentle feedback. No failure sounds, no red crosses, no "wrong". A mistake just waits, and the child tries again.
  • No time pressure. The learning games have no timers. A timer appears only to get ready for a change.
  • Low stimulation. Soft, short sounds, no flashing, and a Calm mode. The app respects the device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Calm rewards. One flower per finished activity. Never a random prize, never a streak that can be lost.
  • Works three ways. Without reading (pictures and voice), without sound (everything is also shown), and without fine motor control (large taps).

Getting started

  1. Install the newest calmcrew_<version>.apk from the download folder once it appears (see how testing works).
  2. Hold the tablet sideways. Calm Crew is made for tablets in landscape; phones work sideways too.
  3. Tap a place in the valley and play. Finishing an activity grows a flower, then the child picks Again or Home.

Updating: open the new APK and tap Update. Saved settings and the garden are kept. Don't uninstall first, since uninstalling clears them.

Your data

Calm Crew has no account, no ads, no purchases and no links out, and it makes no network requests. It collects nothing about your child. Settings and Pip's garden are saved only on the device. No microphone, camera or location.
