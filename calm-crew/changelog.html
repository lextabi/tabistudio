What's new in Calm Crew

Every version for testers, newest first. Download the APK from the download folder; the file name has the version in it (calmcrew_<version>.apk). Each version lists its SHA-256 checksum, so you can check that your download is the real file.

0.4.0 (October 16, 2026)

Download: calmcrew_0.4.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 SHA256_PENDING

Levels, a new place and recording

  • Pattern Path: a new place in the valley. Mochi shows a row of shapes and the child picks what comes next, three patterns per round.
  • Levels: Counting Garden goes up to 5, 8 or 10 flowers, Word Treehouse has 3-, 4- and 5-letter words (14 new words), and Pattern Path has three levels.
  • Hear yourself in the Echo Shop: with the microphone turned on, the child can record a word and hear it played back once. Nothing is saved, sent or scored.
  • More voices: 52 new spoken lines, so every new word, number and shape is read aloud.
  • Tidier Counting Garden: the Next button no longer covers a flower bed.

For grown-ups

  • Levels for counting, words and patterns are chosen in Settings (hold the gear button for 3 seconds). They start at level 1 and never change by themselves.
  • Echo Shop microphone is off until you turn it on in Settings; Android then asks for the microphone. See the privacy policy.

Calm Crew still works fully offline and collects no data. It now asks for vibration and, only if you turn it on, the microphone.

0.3.0 (October 4, 2026)

Download: calmcrew_0.3.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 3baa4e966bd5a038158efe50c5cf6da2292ca6acc5026b992d695ea04360d660

The first test version

  • A sunny valley: the home screen is a valley where each activity is a place to visit.
  • Five activities: Counting Garden, Word Treehouse (spelling), Tocky's Echo Shop (speech), Mochi's Cozy Cave (breathing, bubble popping and rain) and My Day (a picture schedule with a countdown).
  • Character voices: Pip, Mochi and Tocky each speak in their own voice, so the app can be used without reading. All 121 spoken lines ship with the app.
  • Pip's garden: every finished activity grows a flower.

For grown-ups

  • Settings open only when you hold the gear button for 3 seconds.
  • Calm mode softens the colors and turns off movement, except the slow breathing bubble.

Calm Crew works fully offline, asks only for vibration, and collects no data.
