Kitchen

0.14.0 (October 10, 2026)

Download: runling_0.14.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 c69a05208f10bd6be57c08b3088ab8d6ebd0ede4ecbf2e765c0d78359e8b598c

Installs over 0.13.0; your runner, progress, friends and house stay.

New: running clubs

  • Open Social → Club to start a club (from level 5, for 1,000 coins) or join one: by its code, or from the list of open clubs. Up to 30 members.
  • Every member earns Club Points each week: mostly from walking (1 point per 100 steps, up to 150 a day), plus races, daily Event medals and bosses you beat.
  • Clubs race in groups in Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. When the week ends (Sunday midnight, Philippine time), the top clubs in each group move up, the bottom ones move down, and the winner gets a trophy for the club's trophy shelf.
  • The club page shows the members first, with everyone's points this week, then your group's standings. The owner can edit the club, make it code-only and hand it over.

New: chat

  • Social → Chat has World, your Club, and a tab for each friend you talk to. Tap Message on a friend's profile to start.
  • Rude words are starred out, and links, emails and phone numbers can't be sent. Only friends can message you privately.
  • World chat: everyone can read it; you can post from level 10 once your account is a week old, one message every 10 seconds. World messages disappear after a day.
  • Tap any message to report it. Messages reported by several players are hidden, and players who keep breaking the rules can't chat for a while.

Fixes

  • The medal rankings were empty; now they show everyone with medals.
  • Your friends now show up in rankings and name search even before they update.
  • Old personal bests from before the real race times no longer show on the rankings.
  • Kitchen: the ceiling lamp no longer hides behind the Race Day Poster.
  • Rankings have a new Club view, and profiles show each runner's club.

0.13.0 (October 10, 2026)

Download: runling_0.13.0.apk from the Google Drive folder · SHA-256 0f8a8bb17255929fa4a3b867693b500b4e2b09eab8fe28fcfa9e3bc87263b9ab

Installs over 0.12.0; your runner, progress and house stay.

New: friends and rankings

  • A new Social tab. Add a friend by typing their runner name exactly (names are unique, so you find the right person), then accept or decline requests.
  • Tap any runner to see their profile: their runner as they're dressed, level, league, Event medals and best times. From there you can add them, remove a friend, block or report.
  • Rankings: league rating, Event medals and best time per distance, for everyone (top 50 plus your own place) or just your friends. Weekly steps are ranked among friends only.
  • Menu → Friends and privacy: choose whether friends see your weekly steps, who can send you friend requests (everyone, friends of friends or nobody), and unblock players.
  • Running clubs and chat are coming next.

Race times are real now

  • Races now run at real-world paces. A new runner does about 6 minutes per km, and even a fully trained, fully geared runner stays just under world-record pace (about 14 minutes for a 5K and 2 hours 14 for a marathon). Longer races are a little slower for everyone, like in real life.
  • Old personal bests were too fast to ever beat, so they're cleared once when you open this version (a card explains it). Your races, podiums and medals all stay.

Guides for every level

  • Everything a new level unlocks now has a short guide card, including new furniture, vehicles, extra daily quests and the level 40 and 50 rewards. A level's cards come one after another ("2 of 4").
  • Show me now takes you to the exact spot: it opens the right tab or room, scrolls there and puts an orange "Here it is" outline around it.

Changes

  • Change password opens its own window: type the new password twice, and it's saved only when both match.
  • Runner names with rude words or insults (English and Filipino) aren't allowed anymore. Existing names stay.
  • The bottom bar has a sixth button, Social.

New: a house you swipe through

  • Your home is now six rooms, one per screen: Bedroom, Kitchen and Medal hall from the start, then the Garage (level 10), Living room (level 15) and Home Gym (level 18). Swipe, or tap the arrows under the room.
  • Every piece of furniture has its own place, so nothing overlaps anymore. Your runner walks over to the room you're looking at.
  • Your Hunger, Thirst, Nutrition and Fitness sit in four small boxes under the room (tap one for details), and your runner tells you when they're hungry or thirsty.

New: 14 pieces of furniture

  • Fruit Bowl, Blackout Curtains, Water Filter, Shoe Rack, Foam Roller, Blender, Race Map Print, Memory Foam Mattress, Weight Bench, Rowing Machine, Training Whiteboard, Exercise Bike, Massage Chair and Treadmill, from level 2 to 30. Each gives a small perk, like shoes that wear slower or an extra point when you train Strength.
  • The Shop's Home list now shows furniture room by room, with the level each piece unlocks.

New: the garage and your vehicle

  • Start with a City Bike at level 10 and trade up: E-Bike (16), Scooter (22) and the Camper Van (30). Each one adds Charisma. Your old vehicle is traded in for part of its price.

New: the medal wall

  • The Medal hall has a wall with one hook per race distance, for Event and boss medals. Each hook shows your best medal there and how many you have, so every medal fits.

Changes

  • Level-ups now add a full bar of energy on top of what you have left, instead of just refilling to full. 50 energy left at a level-up becomes 150.
  • The Race Day Poster moved to the kitchen; the Medal Rack (now showing your three newest medals) and the Trophy Cabinet moved to the Medal hall. Same perks.

  • Every day's Event has its own name, like Harbor Lights 10K or Pine Ridge Marathon, and the same name never comes back for 100 days.
