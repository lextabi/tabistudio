Runling

Your real steps power your runner. Build a runner that looks like you, take care of them, train them and race them. Walk more in real life, and they get stronger.

Download for Android What's new

Android 8.0 or newer, 64-bit phone · Free, no ads, nothing to buy · Needs a free account

What's in the game

Your runner

Pick a body, skin tone, eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth and a hairstyle made for your body type, plus a name that's yours alone. Everything they wear shows on them.

Real steps

Your daily steps from Health Connect set your runner's Fitness and give energy, coins and XP. Walkers always get more out of the game.

Races

From the free Community Fun Run to the Marathon, at real-world paces: nobody beats a world record. The ground (track, road, trail, gravel, mud) changes daily, and every race rolls its own weather: rain favors Luck and Strength, heat favors Stamina.

Friends and rankings

Add friends by their runner name, see their runners, levels and personal bests, and compare league ratings, Event medals and best times with everyone. Weekly steps are compared among friends and club members only, and you choose whether to show yours.

Running clubs

Start or join a club of up to 30 runners. Every member's steps, races and medals earn Club Points, and each week clubs climb from Bronze to Diamond, with trophies for group winners.

Chat

Chat with your friends, your club and the whole world. Rude words are starred out, links and phone numbers are blocked, only friends can message you privately, and any message can be reported.

Daily Event

Once a day, race everyone who registers. Each day has its own name, like Harbor Lights 10K, that doesn't repeat for 100 days. Bigger prizes, gear for the podium, and medals with the Event's name and date.

Boss challenges

Race one on one against five boss runners, one for each distance. Beat a boss once for a big reward and a Challenge medal. Bosses never get stronger: you do, by walking and training.

Home

Swipe through a six-room house: bedroom, kitchen, medal hall, and later a garage, living room and home gym. 24 pieces of furniture each give a small perk, a vehicle in the garage adds Charisma, and your runner potters about wherever you look.

Quests and medals

Daily and weekly quests with a bonus for finishing them all, Perfect Month, a monthly walking milestone, a login calendar that never resets, and race bibs with your personal bests. A short guide shows you everything each new level unlocks.

Getting started

  1. Install the newest runling_<version>.apk from the download folder (see how testing works).
  2. Create an account. We email you a code to type into the game; check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a minute.
  3. Make your runner, then follow the short "Getting started" guide: feed them, run your first race, train.
  4. Connect your steps: tap Connect steps and allow Steps in Health Connect (built into Android 14 and newer; on older phones, install Health Connect from Google Play first).

Updating: open the new APK and tap Update. Your runner is saved to your account, so you stay logged in and keep everything, even on a new phone.

Your data

Runling keeps your account and game progress on its server so nothing is lost when you change phones. It reads only your daily step totals from Health Connect, and only if you allow it. Other players see only your public profile (runner name and look, level, league, club, medals and best times); friends and club members can also see your weekly step total if you allow it, and chat messages go to the people in that chat. No location, no ads, no tracking, and your information is never sold.
