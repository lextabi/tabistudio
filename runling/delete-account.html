Delete your Runling account

You can delete your Runling account (by Tabi Studio) and all its data at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Runling and log in.
  2. Tap Menu, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email (if you can't open the game)

Email tabistudio.dev@gmail.com from the email address you use for Runling, with the subject "Delete my Runling account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything: your login (email and password), your runner and its looks, stats, coins, items, house, quests, step history and race results. None of it is kept after deletion, and it can't be restored. Short-lived server logs expire on their own after a short period.

Deleting your account doesn't remove the step data stored on your phone by Health Connect; you manage that in Health Connect itself.

See also the Runling privacy policy.
