Runling Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026, updated for Runling 0.14.0 (first version 28 September 2026)

Runling is a game for Android made by Tabi Studio ("we"). Your real daily steps power your runner in the game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we use your email to keep your account, your daily step totals to run the game, and a small public profile so you can add friends and see rankings. We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and never sell or share your information.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Email address and password To create your account, log you in, and send you account emails (sign-up confirmation codes and password reset codes). Your password is stored only in hashed form; we can't read it.
Daily step counts (from Health Connect, only if you allow it) To run the game: your steps set your runner's Fitness and give energy, coins and experience. The game reads step totals per day. It doesn't read any other health or fitness data.
Game data Your runner's name and look, stats, coins, items, house, quests and race results, so your progress is saved in the cloud and works on any phone you log in on.
Public profile Other players can see your runner's name and look, level, league, club, daily Event medals and personal best race times, in the rankings and when they open your profile. Your email and the rest of your game data are never shown to other players.
Friends and your weekly step total Your friend list, and the requests you send or receive. Your total steps this week is shown only to your friends, and only while "Show my weekly steps to friends" is on (in the game: Menu → Friends and privacy). It's never shown to strangers or on the public rankings.
Clubs The club you're in, when you joined, and the Club Points you earn for it each week (worked out from your steps, races and medals). Club members see each other's weekly points; a member who hides their weekly steps shows no points to others.
Chat messages The messages you send, so the people in that chat can read them: world chat (everyone), your club, or a friend. World messages are deleted after 24 hours, other messages after 30 days. Messages are checked when sent: rude words are starred out and links, emails and phone numbers are refused.
Blocks and reports The players you block, so they can't find you, send you requests or show in your chats, and the reports you send (the reason, and the reported runner name or a copy of the reported message), so we can review them and keep the game friendly. Reviewed reports are deleted after 30 days.
Time zone To start each new game day at your local midnight.
Technical logs Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and request times) to keep the service secure and working. We don't use them to identify or track you.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID.

Health Connect

Runling asks Health Connect for permission to read steps only. You can refuse, or remove the permission at any time in Health Connect settings; the game then keeps working without real steps. Step data is used only to provide the game's features described above. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, except the hosting provider that stores it for us and, if you choose to, your weekly step total with your friends in the game (you can turn this off at any time in Menu → Friends and privacy).

Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who handles your information

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. These service providers process it for us, only to run the game:

  • Supabase hosts your account and game data. Its servers are in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the account emails we send you.
  • Google Play distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, runner, public profile, step history, race results, friends, blocks and the reports you sent are removed from our database straight away, together with your club membership and the chat messages you sent. Server logs expire automatically after a short period.

Deleting your account

In the game, open Menu → Delete account. If you can't open the game, see how to request deletion by email.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn step access on or off at any time in Health Connect.
  • Choose whether friends see your weekly steps, and who can send you friend requests (everyone, friends of friends, or nobody), in Menu → Friends and privacy. Block or report any player from their profile.
  • Change your password in the game (Menu).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). The game's rules and your data live on the server. Each player can reach only their own data; other players see only the public profile described above.

Children

Runling isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
