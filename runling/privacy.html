Runling Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Runling is a game for Android made by Tabi Studio ("we"). Your real daily steps power your runner in the game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we use your email to keep your account, your daily step totals to run the game, and nothing else. We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and never sell or share your information.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Email address and password To create your account, log you in, and send you account emails (sign-up confirmation codes and password reset codes). Your password is stored only in hashed form; we can't read it.
Daily step counts (from Health Connect, only if you allow it) To run the game: your steps set your runner's Fitness and give energy, coins and experience. The game reads step totals per day. It doesn't read any other health or fitness data.
Game data Your runner's name and look, stats, coins, items, house, quests and race results, so your progress is saved in the cloud and works on any phone you log in on.
Time zone To start each new game day at your local midnight.
Technical logs Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and request times) to keep the service secure and working. We don't use them to identify or track you.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID.

Health Connect

Runling asks Health Connect for permission to read steps only. You can refuse, or remove the permission at any time in Health Connect settings; the game then keeps working without real steps. Step data is used only to provide the game's features described above. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, except the hosting provider that stores it for us.

Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who handles your information

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. These service providers process it for us, only to run the game:

  • Supabase hosts your account and game data. Its servers are in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the account emails we send you.
  • Google Play distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, runner, step history and race results are removed from our database straight away. Server logs expire automatically after a short period.

Deleting your account

In the game, open Menu → Delete account. If you can't open the game, see how to request deletion by email.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn step access on or off at any time in Health Connect.
  • Change your password in the game (Menu).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). The game's rules and your data live on the server, and each player can reach only their own data.

Children

Runling isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
