How testing works

Our apps aren't on Google Play yet. While they're in open testing, you install them straight from our Google Drive folder:

  1. Open the app's download folder on your phone and tap the newest .apk file (the highest version number). If Drive says it can't scan the file for viruses because it's large, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  3. Tap Install. For a new version later, do the same and tap Update; your data is kept.

Testing means the apps are still growing: things may change between versions, and your feedback shapes what comes next. Each app's page lists what's new in every version.
