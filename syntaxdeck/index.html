SyntaxDeck

An offline cheat sheet for coders. Look up a command, see real examples with their real output, and copy what you need. No sign-up, no internet needed.

Download for Android What's new

Android 7.0 or newer · Free · No account needed

What it does

Linux essentials

147 everyday commands: files, text, permissions, processes, networking, archives, packages and services.

Bash

46 cards on the shell itself: pipes and redirects, &&, history, aliases, variables, loops and scripts.

Real, tested examples

Every example was run on Linux, and its output is shown as it really appears.

Fast search

Results as you type, typos fixed for you, and symbols like 2>&1 or $? work too.

Danger warnings

Commands that delete or overwrite things are marked, with a plain note on what can go wrong.

Copy and share

Copy any syntax line or example with one tap, or share a card through your phone's share menu.

Favorites

Star the cards you use often, and find the ones you opened recently in Saved.

Light and dark

Follows your phone's theme, and the code text size is yours to choose.

More languages (PowerShell, Python, Git and others) come in later versions.

Getting started

  1. Install the newest syntaxdeck_<version>.apk from the download folder (see how testing works).
  2. Search for a command or a task, or open Browse to look through a pack.
  3. Tap the star on a card to keep it in Saved.

Updating: open the new APK and tap Update; your favorites stay. Don't uninstall the old version first: uninstalling deletes your favorites and history.

Your data

  • SyntaxDeck collects no data. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.
  • It asks for no permissions and doesn't use the internet: all cards are inside the app.
  • Your favorites and recently viewed cards are saved only on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • When you share a card, your phone's share menu sends it to the app you pick. SyntaxDeck sees nothing of it.
